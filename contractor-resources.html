Copy the useful structure into the system you already use. These examples do not confirm consent, diagnose a caller, reinterpret an estimate, approve a customer message, or authorize an automated send.

Missed-call handoff record

  • Source event, time, approved business line, and duplicate key
  • Known test, spam, blocked, emergency, and wrong-number exclusions
  • Permission basis and approved acknowledgement wording
  • Assigned person, response window, next action, and stop reason

Draft only: “Thanks for calling [Business]. We missed you. If you intended to contact us about [supported scope], reply with the service, city, and timing a person should review. Reply STOP to end texts.”

A business must verify the recipient, context, applicable law, provider rules, and exact wording before sending anything.

Estimate follow-up

Keep delivery, questions, decisions, and money separate.

Track quote ID, delivery evidence, current version, valid-through date, open question, named owner, next action, stop reason, decision evidence, job status, and collected revenue. Silence is not approval, rejection, a lost job, or revenue.

Draft only: “Hi [Name]—checking that estimate [ID] reached you. If anything is unclear, reply with the question and [Owner] will review it. No decision is assumed.”

Three operating questions

What this checklist is—and is not.

What should a contractor record after a missed call?

Record the source event, time, business line, exclusions, permission basis, assigned human owner, next action, and stop conditions before connecting any automated acknowledgement.

What belongs in an estimate follow-up tracker?

Keep quote delivery evidence, current status, valid-through date, open questions, named owner, next action, stop reason, decision evidence, job status, and collected revenue separate.

How should a contractor ask for an honest review?

Use one neutral milestone for comparable customers, ask for an honest review without prescribing sentiment, offer a private support route, and do not gate, buy, fabricate, suppress, or condition the request on a positive rating.
