Prepare one small repair before the contractor conversation.

Use this page to organize observations, photos, quote questions, changes, and closeout. It is an organizer—not a diagnosis, estimate, contractor verification, permit decision, contract review, or payment approval.

Step 1

Write only what you can observe.

Location

Name the room, side of the house, fixture, gate, deck section, or other exact area.

Condition

Describe what you see, hear, smell, or can operate. Do not guess at the cause.

Timing

Record when you noticed it, whether it changed, and any real deadline or access constraint.

Boundary

Keep unsafe conditions, emergencies, and diagnosis with the appropriate qualified person.

Step 2

Take photos that explain the area.

Wide

Show the full area and how it connects to the property.

Medium

Show where the visible condition sits inside that area.

Close

Capture visible details or safe measurements without entering a hazardous area.

Access

Show gates, stairs, clearances, obstructions, and staging constraints; exclude private details.

Steps 3–4

Compare the written work, then record changes and closeout.

Scope

What exact work and materials are included—and excluded?

Process

What timing, access, cleanup, warranty, change-order, and payment terms are written down?

Changes

Record each proposed change, written price or schedule effect, and actual approval evidence.

Closeout

Walk the visible scope, list unresolved items, and retain the documents actually received.
