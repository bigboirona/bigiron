Questions 1–3

Define the work before comparing the number.

01 · Included

What exact work is included, by location and task?

Look for observable areas and actions—not a vague label such as “repair as needed.”

02 · Excluded

What is specifically excluded or still unknown?

Unknown concealed conditions should stay identified as unknown rather than silently priced as certain.

03 · Responsibility

Who handles protection, access, moving items, disposal, and cleanup?

Write down owner responsibilities as clearly as contractor responsibilities.

Questions 4–6

Make materials and timing visible.

04 · Materials

Are material type, grade, finish, quantity, or allowance identified?

If a selection is pending, record who selects it and how a price difference will be handled.

05 · Schedule

What start window, working time, and completion assumption are written?

A schedule can depend on access, weather, material availability, inspections, or discovered conditions.

06 · Access

What property access, parking, staging, pets, utilities, or occupied-area limits matter?

Surface those constraints before work begins instead of discovering them mid-project.

Questions 7–9

Control price changes and payment expectations.

07 · Price

Is the written amount fixed, estimated, time-and-materials, or allowance-based?

Use the language actually provided; do not assume every total represents the same pricing structure.

08 · Changes

How are additional work, price, and schedule effects documented before proceeding?

Keep a written record of the proposed change and the actual approval evidence.

09 · Payments

What deposit, milestone, final-payment, and accepted-payment terms are written?

Verify current contract and payment rules with the relevant government source for the project location.

Questions 10–12

Plan the finish before work starts.

10 · Warranty

Is any warranty language written, with coverage, exclusions, and contact steps?

Do not infer a promise that is not actually in the documents.

11 · Closeout

What walkthrough, cleanup, unresolved-item, and final-document steps are included?

List the visible completion evidence you expect to review together.

12 · Questions

Which gaps must be answered in writing before you decide?

A clean comparison highlights missing facts; it does not rank contractors or choose a bid for you.
