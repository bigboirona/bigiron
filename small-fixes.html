Clear scope, straight answer. Big Iron Handyman LLC is not licensed by the California Contractors State License Board. We only consider work with a total price less than $1,000, including labor, materials and all other costs, that requires no permit and in which no other person performs or assists with the work. We do not split a larger project into smaller jobs to fit this limit. Work outside it needs an appropriately licensed contractor.
