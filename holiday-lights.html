A few practical details.

Can I use lights I already own?

Yes. We review their condition and suitability for a seasonal display. They remain yours, and Big Iron storage is included in your quoted annual plan. If you prefer to store them at home, ask for a custom setup and removal quote.

Is it one fixed price for every house?

No. A short entryway display and a large roofline need different materials, equipment, and time. Your written quote separates the products and service you choose.

What does annual service include?

Your written plan names the holidays, lights, display, one setup and removal per covered season, and Big Iron storage. The lights belong to you. We plan our return and reconfirm next season's dates, work and price under your annual terms. Each season follows its agreed terms and price. Customer storage receives a custom quote. This request does not start a subscription or authorize a charge.
