A few practical details.

Can I use lights I already own?

Yes. We review their condition and suitability for the display, then quote the setup and takedown. You can store them yourself or ask us to include storage.

Is it one fixed price for every house?

No. A short entryway display and a large roofline need different materials, equipment, and time. Your written quote separates the products and service you choose.

What does annual service include?

Your plan names the holidays, display, installation and removal windows, and storage choice. We agree on future-season arrangements and any changed scope with you. This request does not start a subscription or authorize a charge.
