Questions, answered

Common questions before booking or requesting a quote.

These answers explain the live request, rough-range, confirmation, project-prep, and follow-up boundaries before you send job details.

Do I pay when I submit a booking request?

No. The website sends a booking request to Big Iron and does not collect payment in that flow. Any price, deposit, payment schedule, and accepted method must be confirmed separately in the applicable written estimate or agreement.

Is a booking request a confirmed appointment?

No. The selected time is a requested window. Big Iron reviews the service, location, scope, and availability before confirming or proposing a different time.

How should I request a custom or complicated scope?

Use the custom quote form for larger jobs, multi-item repair lists, or work that needs tailored review. Include observable conditions, location, timing, access constraints, and safe photos when available without guessing at the cause.

Is the rough quote tool a final estimate?

No. It is a preliminary range based on selected categories and assumptions. It is not an inspection, diagnosis, bid, contract, guaranteed price, schedule, or promise that Big Iron will accept the project. Open the rough-range tool.

How can I prepare useful project details and photos?

Describe what you can observe, capture wide and context photos before close-ups, keep private details out of the frame, and compare written scope rather than price alone. Use the free project-prep guide and 12-question estimate checklist.

Can I use AI to organize home-project questions safely?

AI may organize redacted observations and questions, but it cannot authenticate a contractor, inspect or diagnose a condition, decide whether an area is safe, review a contract, determine permits, set a fair price, or approve payment. Keep private details out and verify important claims independently. Use the free plain-language AI safety checklist.

How can I change or cancel a requested time?

Call or text (831) 291-3272 as soon as possible. A requested window can be adjusted during follow-up before it is confirmed; any later change depends on the actual project and scheduling commitments.
