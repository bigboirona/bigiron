Privacy notice

How this website handles information

Effective August 14, 2026. This notice describes information handled through the Big Iron Construction website.

Information you provide

When you request a quote or booking, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, service address, project details, preferred timing, and access notes. We use that information to review your request, contact you, prepare a quote, schedule work, and maintain our customer records.

Optional website analytics

If you select “Accept analytics,” this site loads Google Analytics 4. Google Analytics may collect page and session activity, referring-page information, approximate location, and browser and device information. It uses first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers. Big Iron has disabled Google signals and ad-personalization signals in this site’s configuration.

We send a form-completion event after the website confirms delivery, but we do not send the name, email address, phone number, address, or project message from the form to Google Analytics. You can decline analytics and still use the site.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Browser storage

The site may store your analytics choice, advertising attribution details such as UTM parameters or click identifiers, and an unfinished quote summary in your browser. Contact and booking form details are not saved to browser storage by this site. You can clear stored information through your browser settings.

Service providers and disclosures

This website uses GitHub Pages for website hosting, Cloudflare for secure delivery of form requests, Google Analytics when accepted, and Big Iron’s internal customer-management system to receive requests. These services process information as needed to provide, protect, measure, and operate the website. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, customers, or business.

Your choices and questions

Change your analytics choice. The site does not separately respond to a browser’s “Do Not Track” signal; the analytics choice shown on this site controls whether Google Analytics loads. For questions or requests concerning website information, call or text (831) 291-3272 or email bigironhandyman@gmail.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website and its service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
