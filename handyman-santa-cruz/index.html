Repairs that make sense for coastal homes

Exterior wood, trim, railings, decks, and gates in Santa Cruz take steady exposure to moisture and coastal air. Big Iron handles deck repair, fence repair, gutter and roofline work, siding patches, fascia, trim replacement, and coordinated exterior repair scopes that need practical carpentry and a clean finish.

We work with homeowners around Santa Cruz, including the Westside, Eastside, Seabright, Live Oak, Pleasure Point, and nearby neighborhoods where homes deal with weather, older materials, and deferred maintenance. Some calls are for one obvious issue. Others are for a whole exterior list that has built up over time and needs to be handled by somebody who can actually move through it.

From gates and decks to trim and gutters

We handle deck boards, railing resets, gates that sag or bind, failing exterior trim, gutter trouble, siding damage, and small exterior builds that improve function. The approach stays direct: review the photos, understand the scope, and recommend the next step based on what the property needs.
